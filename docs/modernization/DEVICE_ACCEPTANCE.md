# Device acceptance protocol (unperformed)

Use a disposable test device or an explicitly authorized matching-certificate upgrade. Keep an inspected backup; never uninstall/clear the user's real installation to bypass a mismatch. Record model, Android/Honor version, certificate, prior version, theme, language, direction, font scale and results.

1. Upgrade a matching-signature installation containing two calendars, hidden historical shifts, each shift kind, overlapping/multiple shifts, tasks/reminders, notes, payments/allocations, obligations, PIN settings and appearance profiles. Compare IDs, counts and amounts before/after; restart. Check that a failed/corrupt read blocks writes rather than initializing a blank store.
2. Calendar: toggle visibility independently of primary. Exercise every shift type with custom paint on/off, outside dates on/off, all three cell sizes and month/week/day. Check single/multiple workplace colors and legible notes/date/today/selection in Light/Dark/AMOLED and 1.0/1.5/2.0 font scale. Hide then reshow a populated calendar and verify unchanged records.
3. Painting: open workplace strip, choose type, tap blank day, close Done, open normal details. Bulk-select contiguous/noncontiguous dates; cancel conflict; confirm whole-segment replacement and inspect unaffected shifts/notes. Replace OFF/work, undo, restart and verify remembered selection. Confirm visual bands never change historical time/pay. Closed months must refuse painting until audited reopen.
4. Navigation: arrows and swipes across leap February/December; check clamping and Arabic/English month names. Try UI/nav/grid/weekday direction independently and each first weekday, then change language without changing saved direction. Swipe inside horizontal controls and painting must not navigate accidentally.
5. Appearance: preview/apply/discard/reset only appearance; save/load multiple profiles with different directions/animations while preserving active language. Compare fade/slide/flip/zoom/stack/vertical at each speed; enable Reduce Motion/manual lightweight/automatic reduction/focus and ensure no unwanted saved-preference mutation.
6. Tasks: compact quick add, date/time picker, advanced priority/repeat/reminder/workplace/calendar/shift, keyboard Save, double tap, edit/cancel and unsaved guard. From widget and launcher shortcut, add in floating Activity, cancel, add with denied/granted notification permission, background/rotate/restart; confirm widget refresh and no full MainActivity flash. Check recurring/completed/deep-linked task flows.
7. Notes: add several on an empty date and a shifted date; pin/star/warning, colors/borders, toggle text/icons/name/task dots independently, search/navigate/edit. Erase/copy shifts and verify notes survive and are never bulk copied. Test note save/storage errors without false success.
8. Finances: hide/unlock/lock and verify no amount/search/export/financial undo leaks. Enter every approved ASCII/Arabic/Persian/mixed amount example plus invalid grouping/NaN/negative/huge input. Check inline errors, rounding, keyboard, duplicate-submit guard and all conditional obligation types. Allocate one received amount over several months; sum must match original exactly and not count twice. Edit amount and recheck allocation sources/months.
9. Smart tools: copy ranges with explicit conflicts and cancellation, templates on repeated date/after edit, favorite selection, confirmed next-day suggestion, compare two months and independently calculate totals. Export workplace statement, including source-attributed receipts, and open in a CSV reader. Add past/current/future rates and restart; historical values/exceptional overrides remain correct. Review close/reopen audit and approved health repairs.
10. Backup/security: current/older archives, optional metadata, task/note PNG/JPEG/PDF/text attachments; preview/counts/checksums and exact confirmation. Reject path traversal, excessive size/unsupported content/corrupt hashes. Interrupt restores in a disposable fixture only and verify rollback before load. Check PIN/biometric and financial screenshots/widgets remain protected. Inspect pre-change safety copy before major action and verify current state cannot be overwritten by a stale UI.
11. Notifications/widgets: category/quiet-hour settings, Android permission and exact alarm screens, app background/doze/reboot/time-zone changes, Honor battery policy and launcher sizes. Confirm essential reminders continue when focus/performance changes. Record any OS restrictions rather than treating scheduling as delivery proof.
12. Accessibility/performance: TalkBack labels/focus order, minimum targets, long mixed Arabic/English workplace/task names, small landscape window and large fonts, keyboard overlays, theme contrast, rapid navigation and large synthetic dataset profiling. Optional usage stats must stay local and exclude private content.

Record per-item pass/fail and evidence back into TRACEABILITY.json. A source-review status must not be promoted to accepted merely because the APK builds.
