# Before publishing these policy drafts

- Verify the legal operator name and support email; neither was found in the supplied project.
- Define target jurisdictions, final effective date and enforceable terms with an appropriate reviewer.
- Host all eight HTML files together at public HTTPS URLs and test the relative links and mobile RTL/LTR.
- Enter the verified privacy/terms/support URLs in Google Auth Platform and Google Play where required.
- Recheck every statement against the built application, Android system backup configuration and enabled SDKs.
- Do not remove the draft marker until missing details, actual behavior and final policies have been reviewed.
- Before enabling Google, define and implement retention, backup deletion, account disconnection and revocation; update Google data disclosures and any applicable account deletion requirements.
- No cloud SDK, OAuth credential or archive encryption is implemented by these policy files.
