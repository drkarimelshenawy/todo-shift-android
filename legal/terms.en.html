Purpose

ToDo Shift helps organize shifts and tasks and record amounts, payments and obligations. It does not transfer money or run payroll disbursements and is not a substitute for employer records or accounting review.

Input responsibility

Enter accurate data and review shifts, rates, periods and settlements before relying on results. Missing or incorrect inputs can affect outputs. Keep independent copies of important records.

Device and accounts

You are responsible for protecting your device, lock credentials and exported documents. Do not share private information without authority. Local use does not require Google sign-in.

Backup and restore

A confirmed restore replaces the included local data. The new restore flow validates the file and presents a summary first. Do not restore files of unknown origin. Lock credentials do not transfer through this export; configure protection on the destination device.

Future services

Disabled Google and Drive controls do not provide an active cloud service. Later integration requires configuration, testing and updated policies. Snapshot backup is distinct from multi-device synchronization.

Compatibility and limits

Widget appearance, periodic timing and behavior can vary with Android version, hardware, launcher and battery policy. Visual polish does not guarantee data correctness or readiness of an untested build.
